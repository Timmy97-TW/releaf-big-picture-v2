One call, one plot, one week

What it would actually do, on a Tuesday.

One plot in the band our own map puts at the top of the volatility index, which runs down the west coast through 雲林 and 嘉義 and is dense with parcels under two hectares. Below is the week that follows a single call, with every number carrying where it came from.

  1. T minus ?

    The forecast

    The controller reads a soil sensor and an online forecast together and calls the stress early. How much warning that buys is the number the whole week turns on.

    not on recordForecast lead time. It fills the first season the controller is run against real weather.
  2. T = 0

    The call

    Green light goes on over the chamber at 520 nm, and the intent is that brightness sets the dose rather than switching it on and off. What the controller does with a prediction, threshold or schedule or dose policy, has not been written down.

    not on recordThe dose policy.
  3. ~105 min

    Half maximum

    Published half-maximum for a switch of this class is roughly 105 minutes. Borrowed, not ours: a different organism at a different wavelength, and it is not on record whether that figure describes transcript, protein inside the cell, or anything in the permeate.

  4. hours

    Into the permeate

    The gate we set ourselves is 1 mU/mL of ACC deaminase in neat, cell-free permeate. It is not an arbitrary number. It is at once the ACC-cleaving capacity of a 10⁸ CFU/mL colonisation by Pseudomonas putida UW4, the reference strain in that field, and the activity that gives a 39 hour clearance half-life in the reservoir. With a 5 L reservoir and 60 mL/h of permeate the reactor has to hand over 2.4 mU/mL.

    not on recordThe permeate rate. It is the one input that closes the dilution table, and hardware owes it.
  5. hours to days

    In the root zone

    Clearance runs first order and does not depend on how much ACC is present, because KM is 3.4 mM (Hontzeas 2004), three orders of magnitude above anything a root zone holds. So we never had to guess a concentration nobody has measured. One risk sits directly under that: the same paper reports almost no activity below pH 6.5, and hydroponic solution is normally held at 5.5 to 6.5.

  6. the plant

    Nothing here yet

    No plant has received anything from this system. When one has, the claim is not that it worked once. It is that the same machine does it again on Thursday, and something else next season.

One comparison decides the whole week, and we have not made it.

The forecast has to warn us earlier than the system takes to answer. Induction, translation, secretion, permeate, reservoir, root zone. End to end that is necessarily longer than 105 minutes, and we have measured neither it nor the lead time it has to beat. If the warning is shorter than the answer, the protein arrives after the stress, and the argument for predicting anything falls over.

not on recordEnd-to-end latency, and forecast lead time. Two measurements, and they are the two this page most wants.

If it spread

Where a machine like this would land.

The argument the map makes is not that Taiwan is hot. It is that the farms least able to buy their way out of a bad season are sitting on the worst of it.
